Phone/tablet, landscape. Pass/fail by **feel**.

## What’s new

- Convert: camera **stays on the bird**. No disappearing, no black screen, no crazy HUD numbers
- Tilt stays **live** through ~5s gaps (convert load). Then slew back, same Cal
- F-35 **roars**. Osprey still slaps

## Good feels like

1. Osprey → Fly. Climb. Slide **NAC** down (convert). Bird **stays in view**. HUD stays sane.
2. Same on **F-35** with **VEC** down. STOVL convert: still on camera.
3. If something glitches: **SIM FAULT — Reset** (or Hangar). Not a black freeze.
4. **Tilt ON** + Cal → convert hard. Chip stays **Tilt · live** for short gaps. Phone only — laptop has no gyro.
5. Eyes closed: F-35 roar/scream ≠ Osprey thump.
6. **Settings** still pauses; Close still does **not** Resume. Engines silent in menu.

## Shout if (fail)

- Convert: bird slides off the back of the screen and stays gone
- Black sky + HUD 1e308 / Infinity
- Tilt dies at convert and **stays** no-signal after you tilt the phone again
- F-35 still a thin mosquito whine
- Settings doesn’t pause / Close auto-resumes
